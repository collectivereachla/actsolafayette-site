Accessibility

Last updated September 6, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use actsolafayette.org: people who navigate by keyboard, who use a screen reader, who need larger text or higher contrast, or who prefer less motion. We build to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. Every image that carries meaning has a text alternative, every form field has a visible label, every page can be used with a keyboard alone, colors are checked for contrast, and animation respects your system’s reduced-motion setting.

If something is in your way

Tell us. Email josiah@actsolafayette.org or call (337) 366-0397 and say which page and what happened. We will fix what we can quickly and, in the meantime, get you the information or service another way.
