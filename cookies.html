Cookie Policy

Last updated September 6, 2026

The short version

actsolafayette.org sets no cookies. There is no analytics, no advertising, no embedded social widget, and no third-party script on our pages. Fonts are served from this site, not from Google, so no request for a font reaches a third party. Because nothing here tracks you, there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Where cookies can appear

  • Hosting. Vercel, our host, does not set cookies on visitors of this site.
  • Links elsewhere. Instagram, Calltime, and other sites we link to set their own cookies once you are on them.

If that ever changes

If we ever add analytics or any script that sets cookies, this page will say exactly what it is, why it is there, and how to refuse it, and we will ask for consent first where the law requires it. Questions: josiah@actsolafayette.org.
